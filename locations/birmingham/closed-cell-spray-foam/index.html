Why closed-cell?

The Highest R-Value Per Inch We Install

Closed-cell foam packs the most insulating value into the least thickness, which matters when the cavity is shallow or the space is already finished. It seals air leaks and insulates in a single application, so there is no separate air-sealing step to get wrong.

Because it cures rigid, it also stiffens the assembly it is sprayed into, a real advantage in rim joists, pole barns and metal buildings.

Built for the climate

Moisture Control That Holds Up to Michigan Weather

Closed-cell foam does not absorb water, so it keeps performing in crawl spaces, basements and rim joists where damp would ruin batt or blown-in material.

That moisture resistance, paired with a full air seal, is what stops the warm-air leaks that cause ice dams on the roof through a Detroit winter.
