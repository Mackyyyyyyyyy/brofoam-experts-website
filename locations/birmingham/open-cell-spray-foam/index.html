Why open-cell?

Full Air-Sealing at a Lower Cost

Open-cell foam expands to many times its sprayed volume, so it fills deep cavities fast and reaches into gaps that batts leave open. It delivers the same air seal as closed-cell at a lower price per square foot.

Where the framing is deep enough and there is no moisture concern, it is the most cost-effective way to stop drafts in a home.

Quieter rooms

Sound Dampening as Well as Insulation

The soft, open structure that makes this foam cheaper also makes it good at absorbing airborne sound, which is why it is a popular choice for interior walls, media rooms and floors between levels.

You get a tighter, quieter house from a single application.
