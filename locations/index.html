Metro Detroit Locations

Wayne County

Detroit

Detroit's older housing stock is where we do most of our retrofit work. A lot of these homes were built long before modern insulation standards and still run on thin attic coverage and uninsulated basements. Blown-in and spray foam upgrades here usually pay for themselves fast in a Michigan winter.

Learn More →
Wayne County

Dearborn

Dearborn is home base. Our shop is on W Warren Ave, so it is where we get to fastest. The mix of pre-war bungalows and mid-century homes here means we see everything from empty wall cavities to attics that were never brought up to depth, and we can usually be out for a look the same week.

Learn More →
Oakland County

Birmingham

Birmingham has a lot of older character homes alongside newer high-end builds, and both come to us for different reasons. The established houses need their envelopes tightened up; the newer ones often had the builder spec the minimum, and owners call once the first winter shows them where the heat is going.

Learn More →
Oakland County

West Bloomfield

West Bloomfield is mostly larger residential lots, and the bigger the house the more it costs to heat when the envelope leaks. We do a lot of attic and rim-joist work out here, plus closed-cell foam in the finished basements and bonus rooms that never quite hold their temperature.

Learn More →
Oakland County

Bloomfield Hills

Bloomfield Hills homes tend to be large, custom, and complex to insulate well. Vaulted ceilings, additions and multiple rooflines all create spots where standard batt work leaves gaps. Spray foam earns its place here, sealing the awkward transitions that drive comfort complaints and ice dams.

Learn More →
Macomb County

St. Clair Shores

St. Clair Shores sits right on the lake, so the housing here takes on more wind and damp than most of our area. That combination puts crawl spaces, rim joists and lakeside-facing walls at the top of the list, and closed-cell foam handles the moisture side as well as the heat.
